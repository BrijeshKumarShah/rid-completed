Welcome to the World of Research, Innovation & Discovery (RID)

At RID, we are dedicated to advancing knowledge through research, transforming ideas into innovation, and discovering practical solutions to real-world challenges—empowering technology that makes life easier, smarter, and more meaningful for everyone

What is RID Organization?

RID Organization i.e. Research, Innovation and Discovery Organization is an organization which is run by TWF (TWKSAA WELFARE FOUNDATION). The main objective of which is that in the coming times, the discovery, publication and use of NEW (RID, PMS & TLR) should be done first in Indian culture, civilization and language from this holy land of India.

Important Message: All the students, teachers and intellectuals who are interested in Research, Innovation & Discovery to join this RID organization and by discovering something new (RID, PMS & TLR) to the world with their intelligence, wisdom and talent. Solve people's problems by creating and adopting.
